How to Build Your Doner

  1. Choose chicken, beef or assorted.
  2. Choose standard or large.
  3. Add sauce or extra meat.
  4. Wait about 15–20 minutes.
  5. Eat in or take away, and pay.

This is the assorted doner our team ordered after walking through the five steps. We asked for the standard size with red sauce and extra peppers, and the order was ready in about seventeen minutes. If you order at a busy hour, ask for the sauce on the side and the doner keeps its texture much longer.

Good to Know

The branch works 24/7, but on Fridays there is a break from 12:30 to 14:00. The board also carries a halal certificate.

The official website Doner na Abaya says Сочные донеры от 1795 тг! (“Juicy doners from 1795 tenge!”), but the cheapest one now costs 1895 ₸. Prices change, so check before you go.
