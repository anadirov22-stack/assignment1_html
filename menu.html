How to Build Your Doner

  1. Choose chicken, beef or assorted.
  2. Choose standard or large.
  3. Add sauce or extra meat.
  4. Eat in or take away, and pay.
  5. Wait about 15–20 minutes.

Menu Words

Doner
Grilled meat with vegetables and sauce. Döner means “turning” in Turkish.
Assorted
Chicken and beef together.
Ayran
A cold sour-milk drink.

Good to Know

The branch works 24/7, but on Fridays there is a break from 12:30 to 14:00. The board also carries a halal certificate.

The official website Doner na Abaya says Сочные донеры от 1795 тг! (“Juicy doners from 1795 tenge!”), but the cheapest one now costs 1895 ₸. Prices change, so check the website before you go.
