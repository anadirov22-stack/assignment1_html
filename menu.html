How to Build Your Doner

  1. Choose chicken, beef or assorted.
  2. Choose 1× or 1.5×.
  3. Add extras: extra meat, peppers, red or white sauce.
  4. Eat in or take away, and pay.
  5. Wait about 15–20 minutes.

Menu Words

Doner
Meat from a turning grill with vegetables and sauce. Döner means “turning” in Turkish.
Assorted
Chicken and beef in one doner.
Ayran
A cold sour-milk drink.

Good to Know

The official website Doner na Abaya says Сочные донеры от 1795 тг! (“Juicy doners from 1795 tenge!”). Prices change, so check the website before you go.
