How to Build Your Doner

  1. Choose chicken, beef or assorted.
  2. Choose standard or large.
  3. Add sauce or extra meat.
  4. Eat in or take away, and pay.
  5. Wait about 15–20 minutes.

This is the assorted doner our team ordered at the branch after walking through the five steps above. We asked for the standard size with red sauce and extra peppers, and the whole order was ready in about seventeen minutes, which matches what the staff told us at the counter. The lavash was grilled on both sides, the meat was cut straight from the spit, and the sauce was added last so it did not soak the bread while we carried the order to the table. If you order at a busy hour, ask for the sauce on the side and the doner keeps its texture much longer.

Good to Know

The branch works 24/7, but on Fridays there is a break from 12:30 to 14:00. The board also carries a halal certificate.

The official website Doner na Abaya says Сочные донеры от 1795 тг! (“Juicy doners from 1795 tenge!”), but the cheapest one now costs 1895 ₸. Prices change, so check the website before you go.
