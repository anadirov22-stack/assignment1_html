Important note:

Doner na Abaya is a legendary, iconic street food landmark in Almaty, Astana, Kazakhstan, best known for pioneering the city's late-night doner culture. Located along Abay Avenue, it is famous for its massive portions, signature garlicky white sauce, and spicy lazjan, making it a cultural staple for students and night owls.
