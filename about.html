About the Restaurant

Doner na Abaya is a fast-food restaurant. Our branch is at 51 Mangilik El. It offers doners with different meat, sauces, vegetables, drinks, and takeaway. The restaurant works 24 hours a day. You can also learn about the branch and its offers.

During our visit, the place was clean and cozy. The staff were polite, and I liked the doner and special sauce.

About the Branch

The branch is easy to find. Customers can order at the counter or take food away. Preparation usually takes 15–20 minutes, but waiting can be longer during busy hours.
