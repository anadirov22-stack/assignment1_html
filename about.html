OUR STORY

More Than Just Street Food

Doner na Abaya started with a simple idea: take the classic local doner everyone loves and serve it in a clean, modern, welcoming urban space.

At our Astana branch on 51 Mangilik El Avenue, fast street-food energy meets consistent kitchen quality. Bright neon signs and a comfortable dining room make it a place to sit down, catch up with friends, and enjoy your meal.

The Doner na Abaya Standard

Carved Fresh Off the Spit

We marinate our chicken and beef in-house and roast it on vertical spits, slicing the meat hot only after your order is placed.

The Crispy Lavash Press

Every roll is layered with golden fries, crunchy pickles, and our signature red and garlic-white sauces, then toasted on the grill press until crisp.

Astana's Day & Night Spot

Located on the Left Bank at Mangilik El, our dining room is built for quick weekday lunches, evening meetups, and late-night cravings alike.
