ORDER GUIDE

Order Information

The image is inside this paragraph so the text can flow around the floated image. First, open the Menu page and choose the food you want. You can check the available doners, sizes and prices there. After choosing your food, return to the Order page. The form below asks for your contact information, quantity, date and type of order. If you need delivery, choose Delivery. If you want to take the food yourself, choose Pickup. You can also write extra information in the comment field. Chicken & beef doners are available in different sizes. When you press Submit, the browser processes the form, but the order is not sent to the restaurant because this project does not have a server yet.

How to Order

  1. Choose food from the Menu page.
  2. Fill in your name, email and phone number.
  3. Choose the quantity and date.
  4. Select Pickup or Delivery.
  5. Check your information and press Submit.
