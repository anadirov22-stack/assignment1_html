Order Information

First, open the Menu page and choose the food you want. You can check the available doners, sizes and prices there. After choosing your food, return to the Order page.
Want to know about more?
You need to write your name and phone number first. How to do it? first scroll down and find Order Form, after that you need to fill in the fields blanks
Want to order a lot of Doners, then you need to fill in the fields the blank at the bottom of that information
To select the day and time just press the date button and put the day of your requirement.
Need to delivery to home or somewhere else, then you need to just press the button delivery, if dont need to deliver just press pickup.
Write your requirements for your service, what you want more of ingredients or how you will have a better inner expression about that place.
When you press Submit, the browser processes the form, but the order is not sent to the restaurant yet because our website does not have a server.
