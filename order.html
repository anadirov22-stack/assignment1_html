Order Information

ORDER GUIDE

First, choose your doner, size and quantity. You can also add a drink and extras from the restaurant menu. The form below asks for your contact information, preferred date and time, and whether the order is Pickup or Delivery.

If you need delivery, choose Delivery. If you want to take the food yourself, choose Pickup. You can also write extra information in the comment field. Chicken & beef doners are available in different sizes.

After you press Submit, your order details will be checked and a confirmation message will appear below the form.

Pickup

Choose Pickup if you want to take the food yourself.

Delivery

Choose Delivery if you want the order delivered.

How to Order

  1. Choose chicken, beef or assorted doner.
  2. Choose standard or large size and quantity from 1 to 10.
  3. Add a drink and extras if you want them.
  4. Fill in your name, email and phone number.
  5. Choose the order date, preferred time and Pickup or Delivery.
  6. Check your information and press Submit.
