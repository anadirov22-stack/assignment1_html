Order Information

ORDER GUIDE

First, open the Menu page and choose the food you want. You can check the available doners, sizes and prices there. After choosing your food, return to the Order page. The form below asks for your contact information, quantity, date and type of order.

If you need delivery, choose Delivery. If you want to take the food yourself, choose Pickup. You can also write extra information in the comment field. Chicken & beef doners are available in different sizes.

After you press Submit, your order details will be checked and a confirmation message will appear below the form.

Pickup

Choose Pickup if you want to take the food yourself.

Delivery

Choose Delivery if you want the order delivered.

How to Order

  1. Choose food from the Menu page.
  2. Fill in your name, email and phone number.
  3. Choose the quantity and date.
  4. Select Pickup or Delivery.
  5. Check your information and press Submit.
