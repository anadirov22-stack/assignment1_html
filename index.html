Doner na Abaya

Welcome to our website about Doner na Abaya, a fast-food restaurant in Astana. We chose this restaurant because it is a real place that we can visit and learn more about. During our visit, we collected information about the branch and took photos to use on the website.

About Doner na Abaya

About the Restaurant

Doner na Abaya is a fast-food restaurant that serves different kinds of doner, snacks, and drinks. During our visit, we saw customers ordering food at the counter and eating inside the restaurant. Customers can eat at the restaurant or order food to take away.

About the Project

In this project, we focus on one branch of the restaurant. We gathered information about its location, services, and menu to create this website. Here you can find details about the restaurant, customer reviews, photos, frequently asked questions, and other useful information.

Website Information

We divided the website into pages for the menu, reviews, gallery, order information, FAQ, and the restaurant.

The pages contain the branch information, menu details, customer opinions, photos, and answers to common questions. Each page focuses on a different part of the project.
